Common Questions

Answers to what hotels and restaurants ask us most.

When do I pay?
You pay only when you hire. If none of the candidates we send work out, you don't pay anything.
What if the person leaves after hiring?
If the person you hire leaves within 30 days, we replace them free — no extra fee. You only pay for the new hire if you keep them past 30 days.
How fast do I get candidates?
Within 72 hours of you telling us the role, we send you 3 vetted candidates. Not 30 — just the 3 best matches.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. The price you see is the price you pay. No percentages, no admin fees, no surprises.
What roles do you place?
Chefs, cooks, pastry staff, waiters, waitresses, housekeepers, cleaners, receptionists, front desk, security, laundry, bar staff, managers, and supervisors.
Do you work outside Osogbo and Ilorin?
Not yet. We currently serve hotels and restaurants in Osogbo and Ilorin. If you're outside these two cities, WhatsApp us — we may be able to help depending on the role.

Ready to hire?

Tell us the role. We'll send you 3 vetted candidates within 72 hours. Flat fee on hire. Free replacement if it doesn't work out.
