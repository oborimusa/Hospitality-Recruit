The Story

I started in hospitality in 1987 as a security officer at a hotel in Lagos. Over the next 38 years, I worked my way through almost every part of the business — event management in Lagos and Jos, then HR and Admin in Ilorin, rising to HR/Admin Manager and finally serving as Deputy General Manager (Admin) at Whitefield Hotels, Ilorin for 2 years before I resigned.

Along the way, I also consulted for a handful of hotels — helping them fix their staffing, tighten their operations, and train their teams. That's when I saw the real problem: hotels and restaurants in Nigeria struggle to find reliable, vetted staff. Not because good people aren't out there — but because most recruiters don't know the industry from the inside.

In 2020, I started HospitalityRecruit to fix that. Since then, I've placed over 100 people — chefs, cooks, waiters, housekeepers, receptionists, and hotel managers — with hotels and restaurants across Osogbo and Ilorin.

I'm not a big agency. I don't have a call center. I don't promise 200-person networks or 94% retention rates. What I do is simple: you tell me the role, I send you 3 vetted candidates within 72 hours, and you pay ₦25,000 when you hire. If the person leaves within 30 days, I replace them free.

That's the whole business. And after 38 years in hospitality — including 2 years running Admin as DGM at Whitefield Hotels — I know exactly how to run it.
